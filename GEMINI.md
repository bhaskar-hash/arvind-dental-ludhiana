# Read AGENTS.md before editing

The rules in `AGENTS.md` are mandatory for every change to this repo. The most important:

- No praise words (best, advanced, affordable, painless, guaranteed, 100%), "free" or offers.
- No reviews, testimonials or before/after photos, and no comparisons with other clinics.
- No X-ray charges, and no clinic facts or prices that are not already in `apps/web/src/lib`.
- Dr. Sahu's photo appears only on /about and in the home page's "A note from Dr. Sahu" section, never on the /lp landing pages or in ads.
- Keep the photo-review wording: "Dr. Sahu will personally review your photos and send you a voice message — this is not an automated diagnosis."
